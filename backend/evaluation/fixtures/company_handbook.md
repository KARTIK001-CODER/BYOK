# Company Handbook — RAGForge Technologies

## Company Overview
RAGForge Technologies is a B2B SaaS platform that provides AI-powered knowledge management and retrieval-augmented generation. Founded in 2023, the company is headquartered in Berlin with remote teams across Europe and North America. Our mission is to make organizational knowledge instantly accessible through secure, tenant-isolated AI.

## Work Policy
All employees work hybrid: two days per week in the office, three days remote. Core collaboration hours are 10:00–16:00 CET. The company observes 28 days of paid annual leave in addition to public holidays. Parental leave is 16 weeks fully paid for all parents, with flexible return-to-work scheduling.

## Code of Conduct
Employees must treat colleagues with respect, maintain confidentiality of customer data, and disclose conflicts of interest. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are strictly prohibited and reported via the anonymous ethics hotline at ethics@ragforge.example.com.

## Onboarding Process
New hires complete a two-week onboarding. Week one covers security training, product overview, and development environment setup. Week two involves shadowing a senior engineer and completing a first ticket. The onboarding buddy is assigned by the People team.

## Internal Communication
Engineering uses Slack channels: #eng-general, #eng-rag, #eng-infra, and #incident-response. Product updates are posted in #product-updates every Monday at 09:00 CET. All-hands is the first Thursday of each month at 15:00 CET.

## Data Privacy Commitment
Customer data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Data residency options include EU, US, and APAC regions. The Data Protection Officer can be reached at privacy@ragforge.example.com.

## Career Development
The engineering ladder has levels: Associate (L1), Engineer (L2), Senior (L3), Staff (L4), and Principal (L5). Promotions are evaluated biannually in March and September. Every engineer receives a 2,000 EUR annual learning budget.
