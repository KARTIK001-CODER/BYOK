# Refund Policy — RAGForge Platform

## Eligibility
Customers may request a full refund within 14 days of the initial purchase, provided that usage is below 25% of the allocated quota (API calls, storage, or indexed documents). Pro-rata refunds are available up to 30 days after purchase when usage is between 25% and 50%. No refunds are issued after 30 days.

## Refund Request Process
To request a refund, submit a ticket via the Support Portal with the subject line "Refund Request: [Order ID]". Include the order ID, purchase date, and reason for cancellation. Our billing team responds within 1 business day and processes eligible refunds within 5–7 business days to the original payment method.

## Subscription Cancellation
Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time from the dashboard under Billing → Subscription → Cancel. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle. Users retain access to all features until the cycle ends. Cancellation does not automatically trigger a refund; a separate refund request must be submitted if eligible.

## Trial Period
New customers receive a 14-day free trial with full feature access, including hybrid search and unlimited knowledge bases. No credit card is required for the trial. If the trial is not cancelled, it automatically converts to a paid plan on day 15. Trial data is retained for 7 days after expiration before deletion.

## Non-Refundable Items
Add-on services such as custom onboarding, dedicated onboarding, and professional services fees are non-refundable. Overages due to exceeding quota are billed at the end of the cycle and are non-refundable. Annual plans that have exceeded 30 days are non-refundable.

## Cancellation Fee
Early cancellation of an annual contract incurs a cancellation_fee of 15% of the remaining contract value. This fee is waived if cancellation is due to a verified service defect with SLA breach. The cancellation_fee is documented as one word with underscore for exact system matching.

## Exchange Policy
Customers may exchange a plan within 7 days of purchase if the initial plan was incorrectly provisioned. Contact success@ragforge.example.com for exchange requests. Exchanges are processed as a refund and re-purchase.
